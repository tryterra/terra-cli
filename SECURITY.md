# Security Policy

Thank you for helping us keep the Terra CLI secure!

## Reporting security issues

The security of our systems and our customers' data is Terra's top priority, and
we appreciate the work of security researchers acting in good faith to find and
report potential vulnerabilities.

**Please do not report a vulnerability in a public issue.** Use
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/tryterra/terra-cli/security/advisories/new)
instead, which reaches us privately and keeps the whole conversation in one
place.

It helps to include the version you were running and the steps to reproduce it.
